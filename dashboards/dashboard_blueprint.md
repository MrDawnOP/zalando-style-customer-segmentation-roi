# Power BI / Tableau Dashboard Blueprint

## Page 1: Executive Overview
Cards: Revenue, Profit, ROAS, ROI, Orders, Conversion Rate, Top Channel, Top Customer Segment.
Visuals: Monthly revenue trend, revenue/profit by country, revenue by segment, spend vs attributed revenue.
Filters: Date range, country, channel, device type, product category.

## Page 2: Marketing Performance
Visuals: Spend by channel, ROAS by channel, CPA by channel, campaign performance table, high-spend low-return campaigns.
Table columns: campaign ID, campaign name, channel, spend, revenue, ROI, ROAS, CPA, CTR, conversion rate and budget efficiency score.

## Page 3: Customer Segmentation
Visuals: RFM segment distribution, revenue by segment, CLV by segment, churn risk by segment and discount dependency.

## Page 4: Business Recommendations
Visuals: Budget reallocation table, recommended spend by channel, top growth opportunities, retention recommendation matrix and segment targeting strategy.
