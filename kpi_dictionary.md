# KPI Dictionary

## Marketing KPIs
| KPI | Formula | Business Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Total Spend | Sum of campaign spend | Total marketing investment |
| Total Revenue | Sum of attributed campaign revenue | Revenue linked to campaigns |
| ROI | (Revenue - Spend) / Spend | Profitability of marketing investment |
| ROAS | Revenue / Spend | Revenue generated for each euro spent |
| CTR | Clicks / Impressions | Ad engagement rate |
| CPC | Spend / Clicks | Average click cost |
| CPA | Spend / Conversions | Average acquisition cost |
| Conversion Rate | Conversions / Clicks | Share of clicks converting |
| Revenue per Click | Revenue / Clicks | Monetisation value of traffic |
| Campaign Profit | Revenue Attributed - Spend | Campaign contribution before fulfilment costs |
| Budget Efficiency Score | Weighted score using ROAS, ROI and inverse CPA | Budget ranking metric |

## Customer KPIs
| KPI | Formula | Business Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Customer Lifetime Value | Total customer spend | Historical customer value |
| Average Order Value | Total spend / Number of orders | Revenue per order |
| Purchase Frequency | Orders / Customer tenure years | Buying frequency |
| Repeat Purchase Rate | Customers with more than one order / Total customers | Retention strength |
| Churn-Risk Score | Rules-based score from recency, frequency, discounts and returns | Inactivity risk |
| Days Since Last Purchase | Analysis date - Last purchase date | Recency |
| Discount Dependency | Discount / Gross value | Reliance on promotions |
| Return Rate | Returned orders / Total orders | Profitability risk |
| High-Value Customer Flag | Spend >= 85th percentile | Top customer marker |

## Business KPIs
| KPI | Formula | Business Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | Sum of order revenue | Sales generated |
| Profit | Revenue - Cost - Return handling penalty | Order profit |
| Profit Margin | Profit / Revenue | Commercial efficiency |
| Orders | Count of order IDs | Transaction volume |
| Returns | Count of return flags | Returned orders |
| Net Revenue After Returns | Revenue excluding returned orders | Revenue retained after returns |
| Category Performance | Revenue, profit and return rate by category | Product health |
| Country Performance | Revenue, profit and orders by country | Regional performance |
| Device Performance | Revenue, profit and returns by device | Digital experience performance |
